4. Sensor noise robustness (your Item 4 - no citation currently)

· A 2025/2026 survey directly frames your problem: attribute noise - errors in recorded

classifier's performance, and robustness (stable outputs despite moderate input
perturbation) is a key requirement for ML models in dynamic environments. Good
general citation for framing your noise-injection experiment. arxiv

sensor inputs." arxiv
